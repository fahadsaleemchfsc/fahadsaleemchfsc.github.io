CRM and marketing automation services

  • Implementations

    New builds and rebuilds on HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho and field-service CRMs: object model, pipelines, properties, automation, reporting and training.

  • Migrations

    Moves between CRMs with history, activities and owners intact. Built as resumable pipelines with deduplication and reconciliation, not a one-shot import.

  • Integrations and automation

    APIs, webhooks, Make and n8n between your CRM and marketing, billing, telephony, clinical and data tools.

  • Audits

    Read-only reviews of workflows, data quality, lifecycle and reporting, ending in a prioritised fix list. Nothing changes during an audit.

  • Data quality and reporting

    Deduplication, ownership and association checks, and dashboards that match what the sales team actually does.

  • Fractional RevOps

    Ongoing ownership of the stack, a few days a week or full time on contract, including administration, releases and documentation.

  • AI agents and automation

    Agents that read, draft, check and act inside the CRM, with code for the numbers and a person for the sign-off. Agentforce, Claude and other model APIs, Python.

  • Email deliverability

    Authentication, sending infrastructure, bounce and complaint handling, list hygiene, inbox testing and controlled rollouts, on Amazon SES, HubSpot, Salesforce or whatever sends your mail.

How a project runs

Five steps, in this order, on every engagement.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Read-only audit of the system, data and automations. Nothing changes on day one.

  2. 2

    Design

    Object model, naming standard and a phased plan with hours and dependencies.

  3. 3

    Build

    Configuration first, code where the platform stops.

  4. 4

    Test

    Acceptance tests on test records, removed afterwards. Nothing goes live without sign-off.

  5. 5

    Hand over

    As-built reference, user guide and a pilot with a results report.
