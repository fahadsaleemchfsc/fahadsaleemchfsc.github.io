Quote-to-cash for a roofing contractor

Price books, tiered quotes with e-signature, deposits and job costing in a field-service CRM.

Work
Field-service CRM implementation
Platform
Jobber, Field service
Stack
Jobber, Quote templates, Payments, Web forms
Client
Private, by agreement

In one paragraph

A roofing contractor was quoting from memory and spreadsheets, with no deposit discipline and no view of margin until the season ended. In Jobber I loaded the real price lists as products, built good-better-best quote templates with the customer's PDF attached and e-signature in the client hub, set payment schedules with deposits, and set up job costing from real bills so each job shows its margin.

The challenge

A contractor was quoting from memory and spreadsheets, with no deposit discipline and no idea of margin per job until the season ended.

Approach

  • Loaded the real price lists as products so a quote is assembled, not typed.
  • Built good-better-best quote templates with the customer’s PDF attached and e-signature in the client hub.
  • Set job costing to real bills rather than estimates so margin is measured, not assumed.

What was built

  • Price books for each service line, including a partner catalogue at its agreed rate.
  • Quote templates, payment schedules with deposits, and automated follow-ups.
  • Expense codes and receipt upload for job costing.
  • Pipeline views per stage and a website form that records the lead source.

Outcome

Quotes go out in minutes from a template, deposits are collected before work starts, and each job shows its margin from real costs.

Background

Quote-to-cash is the path from a price to money in the bank: quote, approval, deposit, work, invoice, payment. In field-service businesses every step is usually in a different place, and margin is discovered at tax time. Jobber covers the whole path if it is set up around the business's real prices and process.

The problem in detail

Quotes were typed from memory, so they varied between people and days. Deposits were requested inconsistently, so cash arrived late. Costs were not tracked against jobs, so the contractor could not say which kinds of work made money.

How the work was done

The real price lists, including a partner's catalogue at its agreed rate, were loaded as products so a quote is assembled from known items rather than typed. Quote templates offer good, better and best options, attach the customer-facing PDF and collect e-signature in the client hub. Job costing was set to real bills and receipts rather than estimates, so margin is measured.

What was delivered

Price books for each service line. Quote templates, payment schedules with deposits, and automated follow-ups. Expense codes and receipt upload for job costing. Pipeline views per stage and a website form that records where each lead came from.

Results

Quotes go out in minutes from a template, deposits are collected before work starts, and each job shows its margin from real costs.

What this means for your team

  • Load real prices as products. A quote assembled from a catalogue is consistent; a typed quote is a guess.
  • Put the deposit in the payment schedule, so asking for it is not a conversation.
  • Cost jobs from bills, not estimates, or margin is a story rather than a number.

Questions about this project

Is Jobber a CRM?

It is a field-service management platform with CRM functions: clients, requests, quotes, jobs, invoices and payments in one system, with a client hub for approvals and payment.

Can Jobber do tiered quotes?

Yes. Quote templates can present optional line items as good, better and best packages, with the customer choosing in the client hub and signing electronically.

How does job costing work?

Expenses and bills are coded to the job, labour is tracked, and the job shows revenue against real cost so margin is visible per job and per type of work.
