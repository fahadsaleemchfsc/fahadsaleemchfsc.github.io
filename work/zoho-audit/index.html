Zoho CRM health audit and task automation

A health check, a cleanup plan and the automation brief for an energy consultancy’s CRM.

CRM audit and automation brief
Platform
Zoho CRM, Audit
Stack
Zoho CRM, Workflows, Outlook integration

In one paragraph

An energy consultancy's Zoho CRM had grown without an owner: duplicates, unused fields and project handoffs happening in email. I reviewed modules, fields, layouts, automation and data quality in read-only mode, prioritised the cleanup by what blocked the team daily, and wrote the handoff automation as a brief the client's own admin could build from, plus guides for Outlook sync and referral-partner tracking.

The challenge

The CRM had grown without an owner: duplicate records, unused fields, and project handoffs that happened in email rather than the system.

Approach

  • Reviewed modules, fields, layouts, automation and data quality in read-only mode.
  • Prioritised the cleanup by what blocked the team daily rather than by volume.
  • Specified the handoff automation as a brief the client’s own admin could build from.

What was built

  • A health audit with findings and a cleanup plan.
  • A project-handoff and task-automation build brief.
  • A user guide for Outlook sync and referral-partner tracking.

Outcome

The client has a sequenced plan, a brief to build from, and guides that let new staff use the CRM without a walkthrough.

Background

A CRM without an owner accumulates: fields added for one campaign, layouts nobody tidied, workflows nobody remembers. The team works around it, usually in email, and the system stops being the record. An audit is the cheapest way to see the whole state before deciding what to change.

The problem in detail

Duplicate records made reporting unreliable. Unused fields made layouts slow to read. Project handoffs between people happened in email, so the CRM did not show who was responsible for what. New staff needed a walkthrough to use it at all.

How the work was done

Everything was reviewed read-only: modules, fields, layouts, automation and data quality. Nothing changed during the audit. The cleanup was prioritised by what blocked the team every day rather than by volume, so the first fixes were the ones felt most. The handoff automation was specified as a build brief, with triggers, conditions, tasks and owners, written so the client's admin could implement it without me.

What was delivered

A health audit with findings and a sequenced cleanup plan. A project-handoff and task-automation build brief. A user guide for Outlook sync and referral-partner tracking.

Results

The client has a sequenced plan, a brief to build from, and guides that let new staff use the CRM without a walkthrough.

What this means for your team

  • Audit read-only. Changing things while you look at them hides what was there.
  • Prioritise by daily friction, not by record count.
  • Write automation as a brief the client's own admin can build. It keeps the knowledge in the company.

Questions about this project

What does a CRM health audit cover?

Modules and fields, page layouts, automation, integrations, user roles and data quality, with a finding and a recommendation for each area.

Why specify the automation rather than build it?

The client had an admin and wanted the capability in-house. A brief with triggers, conditions and outcomes let them build and own it.

Does Zoho CRM sync with Outlook?

Yes, through Zoho's Outlook integration for email, contacts and calendar. The guide covers setup and the habits that keep it tidy.
