Questions

Why are no clients named?

Every client I work with stays private by default. The work is described by what was built and what it measured, and you get the same discretion.

Which platforms do you work in?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive, Jobber and the marketing automation tools around them. The full list is in the stack section.

Do you work on contract or on projects?

Both. Fixed-scope projects with a written statement of work, or a contract role of up to five days a week.

Can you start immediately?

Yes. The first week is a read-only discovery, so it starts without risk to your live system.

How do you handle access to our systems?

Least access needed, read-only first, and no change made without your approval. Credentials stay with you and are never written into documents.
